# Vertical content strategy — Instagram, reusable on TikTok and YouTube Shorts

**Date:** 2026-08-05 · **Constraint:** discovery. 130 Instagram followers, ~2 website
visitors/week, 4 App Store ratings.

> **Language of this document.** The prose is English, per the repo rule. The scripts
> are **Spanish, verbatim and ready to film** — they are copy, the same exception the
> site's own copy gets. The audience that responds is Spanish-speaking (Instagram
> referrals, and the ES blog out-reads the English originals).

Every claim below is traced to `product-marketing.md` → Hard Constraints. The
"never film" checklist at the end is the part to re-read before each shoot: a false
claim in a video is far harder to retract than one on a page.

---

## The account as it stands — audited 2026-08-05

25 posts · 134 followers · 139 following. What the grid actually contains:

- **It is a cycling-photography account, not a product account.** Of the visible grid,
  one post in eight shows the app. The rest is riding lifestyle — good photography,
  genuinely well shot, and almost all **static 4:5 stills rather than vertical video**.
  That is the whole explanation for 46 website visits in 90 days: stills in the feed
  reach the follower list and stop there.
- **One post is third-party pro-racing photography** (rainbow jersey, UAE, Colnago).
  Remove it. Two problems: the rights are not yours, and it recruits followers who
  came for pro racing and will never install a training app. Follower quality is worth
  more than follower count at this size.
- **The existing strength is real footage.** Whoever is shooting this can already
  produce good cycling b-roll. Use it — see the production note under the pillars.

### Fix the profile before filming anything (≈15 minutes, free)

| Field | Now | Change to | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Name** | `Project Summit` | e.g. `Project Summit · Entreno y recuperación` | Instagram search indexes **username + name only — never the bio**. The name field currently repeats the username and earns nothing. This is the single free keyword slot on the profile. |
| **Category** | `Community` | An app / sport category | "Community" tells the algorithm nothing about who to serve this to, and it is not what the account is. |
| **Bio line 3** | `⬇️ Descúbrela aquí` | The free-tier wedge | "Descúbrela" is a filler line pointing at a link that is already visible. Spend it on the offer. |
| **Bio, overall** | Never says what it is | Say it plainly, and say **iOS** | A stranger arriving from a Reel learns that you crossed Portugal and that it isn't a chatbot — but not that this is an iPhone app for cycling training and recovery. Naming iOS up front also stops Android users bouncing at the App Store. |
| **Highlights** | ¿Qué es? · ¿Cómo va? · Gran Diagonal · Rides | Add one: **Gratis** | The structure is good and `¿Qué es?` is the right first one. The free tier deserves its own, because it is the reason to act today. |

Bio draft, for reference — line 1 and 2 are already strong and stay almost as they are:

> Crucé Portugal en bici. 1000 km sin reventar, con esto.
> App iOS de entreno para ciclistas: la recuperación reescribe el plan.
> Ciencia del deporte, no un chatbot.
> Recuperación y sueño gratis para siempre ⬇️

### The asset already there and barely used: Gran Diagonal

The 1000 km Portugal crossing is the strongest thing this account owns. It is real,
it is the founder's own body on the line, there is already a pinned Reel, a bio line
and a blog post (`gran-diagonal-999km-portugal.html`) — and it is being spent as **one
post**.

A 1000 km crossing is inherently episodic, and it is the bridge between the
photography this account is already good at and the product content it needs:

- The recovery score on the morning of each day, and what it said to do.
- The sleep score across consecutive days of accumulating fatigue — the free tier,
  demonstrated under real load rather than described.
- What broke, what hurt, what the numbers saw coming and what they missed. Saying what
  the engine got *wrong* is worth more than any claim it gets things right.

Six to eight vertical episodes out of a trip already ridden and already filmed. It costs
no new riding, and it is the one series where the product appears without the video
being an ad.

## The premise this is built on

With 130 followers the feed reaches nobody. Posting *to* an audience is not the job;
the job is being **served to strangers by the algorithm**. That single fact decides
almost every choice here:

- The hook has to work for someone who has never heard of Summit, in the first
  1.5 seconds, with the sound off.
- Topics lead, the product follows. Nobody stops for an app they don't know. They
  stop for "your watch said 84 and you woke up four times".
- Reach to **non-followers** is the metric. Likes and follower count are not.
- No logo bumper, no "hola, soy Jordi de Project Summit" intro. Both are reach killers
  at second zero.

The corollary: this is not an audience-nurturing plan. It is a discovery plan that
happens to run on social surfaces.

---

## Four pillars, and the job each one does

| # | Pillar | Job | Share of output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The data conflict** | Reach. Built-in disagreement, needs no product knowledge | 4 of 10 |
| 2 | **Mechanism explained** | Trust. Teaches something true and useful | 3 of 10 |
| 3 | **Product in use** | Intent. The payoff, for people already warm | 2 of 10 |
| 4 | **Founder / building in public** | Credibility. The only social proof that exists | 1 of 10 |

The ratio is deliberately discovery-heavy. Pillar 3 is the one that feels most
productive to make and produces the least reach — keep it at two in ten until the
non-follower numbers say otherwise.

**Pillar 4 matters more than its share suggests.** There are no testimonials, no
usable ratings and no publishable user numbers (`product-marketing.md` → Proof
Points). Founder credibility and mechanism are the only proof available, so the
one-in-ten is not filler. The Gran Diagonal series above is pillar 4 at its best,
and it should be what the first month runs on.

**Production note — play to the footage you already shoot.** Pillars 1 and 2 do not
need a talking head. Voice over your own riding b-roll, with the hook as on-screen
text, is faster to produce, uses the one thing this account is already good at, and
keeps the visual identity continuous with the 25 posts already there. Reserve the
face-to-camera for pillar 4, where the credibility comes from it being you.

---

## Cadence: three a week, filmed in one sitting

A solo founder's content plan dies from cadence, not from quality. So:

- **3 posts/week**, all three recorded in **one 60–90 minute session**, batched.
- Sunday records, publishes Tue / Thu / Sat. Whatever the day, keep it fixed —
  a known publish date is what makes the PostHog spike test readable (below).
- Two weeks of buffer before starting. Publishing from a buffer is the difference
  between a channel and a burst.

Missing a week is fine. Missing the batch session is what ends the programme.

## Format spec (one export, three destinations)

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Aspect / size | 9:16, 1080×1920 |
| Length | 15–30 s (pillars 1, 3) · up to 45 s (pillar 2) |
| Hook | On-screen text within 1.5 s. A claim or a number — never a greeting |
| Subtitles | Burned in, Spanish. Sound-off is the default viewing mode |
| Safe zones | No text in the bottom ~15% or top ~10% — platform UI covers it |
| Branding | Handle at the end only. No logo in the first 3 seconds |

**Cross-posting rules — these are what make one file reusable:**

- **Export clean.** Never post a file carrying another platform's watermark;
  TikTok-watermarked uploads are downranked on both Instagram and Shorts.
- **Same video, different titles.** YouTube Shorts is a *search* surface — give it a
  searchable title (`Por qué tu puntuación de sueño no coincide con cómo te sientes`).
  Instagram and TikTok want the hook as the caption.
- **No "link in bio" on the Instagram video itself.** It suppresses reach in-feed. The
  CTA goes in the caption and the profile link.
- **Shorts is the durable one.** Instagram and TikTok give a spike and decay; Shorts
  accrues through search and is the only one of the three that compounds. Judge it on
  a 90-day window, not a 48-hour one.

## The scripts

Twelve to start — four weeks of buffer at three a week. Each one states what it may
and may not claim.

### Pillar 1 — The data conflict

**1.1 · "84 de sueño"** *(VOC verbatim: Garmin score of 84 after waking four times)*

> **Gancho (texto en pantalla):** «84 de 100. Y te has despertado cuatro veces.»
>
> La mayoría de puntuaciones de sueño comparan tu noche con un ideal genérico. Ocho
> horas, tantos ciclos, tanto profundo.
>
> Pero 6 h 45 no significa lo mismo para ti que para alguien que duerme ocho de
> normal. La pregunta no es si ha sido una buena noche. Es si ha sido una buena noche
> **para ti**.
>
> **Cierre:** Si el número no te dice de dónde sale, vas a acabar ignorándolo.

*Claims:* scoring against your own historical median is real (v1.4 sleep engine,
relative baseline). Do not claim accuracy — claim the question is different.

---

**1.2 · "No se parece a cómo me siento"** *(VOC verbatim, high-intensity)*

> **Gancho:** «Mi puntuación de recuperación no se parece en nada a cómo me siento.»
>
> Esto lo escribió alguien justo antes de dejar de usar su pulsera. Y tenía razón en
> la parte que importa.
>
> Un número que no puedes interrogar solo tiene dos finales: o te lo crees a ciegas, o
> lo ignoras. No hay un tercero.
>
> **Cierre:** Lo que hace falta no es un número más exacto. Es un número que te diga
> qué clase de noche has tenido y por qué.

*Claims:* explainability only. Never claim Summit's score matches feel better.

---

**1.3 · "Descriptivo, no prescriptivo"** *(the objection that cuts against us — answer it)*

> **Gancho:** «Los datos deberían ser descriptivos, no prescriptivos.»
>
> Estoy de acuerdo. Y construyo una app que te dice qué entrenar.
>
> No es una contradicción: lo que no puede pasar es que el número mande sin explicarse.
> Si ves de qué regla sale y con qué datos tuyos, puedes llevarle la contraria **a
> sabiendas**. Eso es distinto de obedecer.
>
> **Cierre:** Escuchar al cuerpo y mirar el dato no son bandos.

*Claims:* traceability to a codified rule (`faq.q2`). This is the documented answer to
the Theme 4 segment. Do not argue the number beats feel.

---

**1.4 · La suscripción que no cambia nada**

> **Gancho:** «Pagas todos los meses por un número que no cambia nada.»
>
> Las pulseras de recuperación puntúan tu descanso. Muy bien. Pero no saben qué
> entrenas mañana, así que ese número nunca mueve una sesión. Es un termómetro de
> suscripción.
>
> Si ya llevas algo que escribe VFC y sueño en Apple Salud, la puntuación de
> recuperación, la de sueño con su desglose completo y la tendencia las tienes
> **gratis, para siempre**. No es una prueba de 14 días. Es el plan gratuito.
>
> **Cierre:** El reloj ya lo tienes. Los datos ya son tuyos.

*Claims:* the free-tier list is exact and complete — recovery score, sleep score +
breakdown, recovery trend, widgets. Nothing else. Say "no es una prueba" out loud;
that conflation is the single most likely false claim. Do not name a competitor.

### Pillar 2 — Mechanism explained

**2.1 · Por qué la forma no es la fitness**

> **Gancho:** «Estás en tu mejor forma del año. Y por eso vas lento.»
>
> Fitness es lo que has acumulado durante meses. Fatiga es lo que has acumulado esta
> semana. La forma es la resta de las dos.
>
> Por eso puedes estar más en forma que nunca y rendir mal: has acumulado sin dejar
> que baje la fatiga. Y por eso el afinamiento no es perder forma — es dejar que
> aparezca la que ya tienes.
>
> **Cierre:** Si nunca bajas la carga, nunca ves para qué la subiste.

*Claims:* general sports science, no product claim. CTL/ATL/TSB is a **Pro** feature —
if the app appears on screen, do not imply it is free.

---

**2.2 · Tu mediana, no un ideal**

> **Gancho:** «Ocho horas no es tu objetivo. Es el de un folleto.»
>
> Un objetivo de sueño genérico te penaliza por una noche que para ti es normal, y te
> premia por una que para ti es mala.
>
> Lo que informa es la distancia a **tu propia mediana**: dónde cae lo de anoche dentro
> de tu rango de las últimas semanas, y cuánta deuda llevas acumulada.
>
> **Cierre:** El punto de comparación correcto eres tú de hace un mes.

---

**2.3 · Base, build, peak, taper en 40 segundos**

> **Gancho:** «Entrenar duro todo el año es la forma más rápida de llegar mal al día
> que te importa.»
>
> Base: volumen fácil, construyes el motor. Build: aparece la intensidad específica de
> tu objetivo. Peak: menos volumen, la intensidad se mantiene. Taper: bajas la carga y
> la forma sale sola.
>
> El error clásico es no tener fases: entrenar siempre al mismo nivel de esfuerzo y
> esperar que el cuerpo distinga.
>
> **Cierre:** No es entrenar más. Es entrenar en el orden correcto.

*Reuse:* this is the existing blog post `periodization-base-build-peak.html` — the
caption links to owned inventory that already ranks in three languages.

---

**2.4 · La recuperación también debería empujar**

> **Gancho:** «Tu app solo sabe frenar.»
>
> Casi todo lo que lee tu recuperación hace una sola cosa: cuando estás hecho polvo, te
> quita carga. Bien. ¿Y cuando estás fino?
>
> Una buena ventana de recuperación es una oportunidad de entrenamiento, y
> desperdiciarla en un día suave cuesta tanto como forzar en uno malo. La adaptación
> tiene que ir en las dos direcciones o solo es un freno.
>
> **Cierre:** Frenar es la mitad del trabajo.

*Claims:* verified on `index.html` ("It reads your recovery — both ways").

### Pillar 3 — Product in use

**3.1 · La mala noche** *(the 30-second demo the press pitches describe)*

> **Gancho (texto):** «Mala noche. Mira lo que pasa con la sesión de hoy.»
>
> [Pantalla: recuperación baja] → [la sesión de hoy, que eran series] → [se convierte
> en rodaje suave] → [se abre el porqué: la regla y los números que la han disparado]
>
> **Cierre:** No ha cambiado porque sí. Puedes leer de dónde sale.

*Claims:* the adaptive plan is **Elite**. Do not let this read as free. Show the
reasoning screen — that is the differentiator, not the change itself.

---

**3.2 · «Hoy tengo cuarenta minutos»**

> **Gancho:** «Hoy no tienes hueco. Tu plan no se entera.»
>
> Le dices el tiempo que tienes de verdad. Cuarenta minutos, y replanifica. Cero, y la
> semana se recoloca alrededor.
>
> Lo que no pasa es que te marque el día en rojo por haber tenido vida.
>
> **Cierre:** Un plan que ignora las semanas malas es una lista de deseos.

*Claims:* availability-driven planning is on `index.html`. "No te marca en rojo" is
aimed at the TrainingPeaks VOC verbatim ("logged as red as missed workouts") —
check before filming that no compliance-red UI exists to contradict it.

---

**3.3 · Lo que es gratis, enseñado entero**

> **Gancho:** «Esto es todo lo que cuesta cero.»
>
> [Recorrido de pantalla, sin cortes] Puntuación de recuperación del día. Puntuación de
> sueño con el desglose completo: dónde cae tu noche en tu rango, deuda, fases, ritmo.
> La tendencia del último mes. Y los widgets en pantalla de inicio y bloqueo.
>
> Gratis permanente, no prueba. Necesitas un iPhone y cualquier pulsera o reloj que
> escriba en Apple Salud.
>
> **Cierre:** Si nunca pagas nada, esto se queda igual.

*Claims:* this list is exhaustive — adding anything else is a false claim. Say the iOS
requirement out loud. **Do not show the app on a watch.**

**4.1 · Por qué existe esto**

> **Gancho:** «Entrenaba para una marcha con cinco apps abiertas y ni una me decía qué

## Never film this

Read before every shoot. These are wrong in the shipped product, and video is the
worst medium in which to be wrong.

- ❌ **The app on an Apple Watch.** There is no watch app in the shipped build. The
  watch is a **sensor**, read through Apple Health.
- ❌ **"IA", "inteligencia artificial", "coach"** as a flat description.
- ❌ **Blurring free with the 14-day trial.** Say "no es una prueba" explicitly.
- ❌ **Anything beyond the four free items** described as free — training load
  (CTL/ATL/TSB), power/eFTP and per-activity analysis are **Pro**; adaptive plan,
  export and nutrition are **Elite**.
- ❌ **Training-driven nutrition plans.** Roadmap, not shipped. Only race-day fuelling exists.
- ❌ **Invented numbers** — users, downloads, ratings, testimonials. None exist.
- ❌ **Implying Android or a web version.** iOS only, iOS 26+ — say the floor out loud
  when the video invites someone to download.

## Measuring it, at this size

No A/B tests, no funnels — there is no volume for either.

1. **Non-follower reach per post** (Instagram gives it natively). This is the number.
   Followers and likes are not.
2. **The spike test.** Publish on a known date and read PostHog day by day. Does any
   single post move website visitors at all? At ~2/week, even five is a visible signal.
3. **After 12 posts, prune.** Keep the pillar with the best median non-follower reach,
   drop the worst, and rebalance the ratio toward what actually travelled. Do not
   rebalance earlier — under 12 posts it is noise.

What this cannot measure: installs. The App Store badge carries a single coarse
`ct=web` token, and Apple only reports a campaign above 5 first-time downloads in 24 h.
Splitting the token per network would keep every one of them under the floor and report
nothing. Leave it alone until download volume justifies it.

---

## Honest caveat

Three posts a week from a 130-follower base does not produce a step change, and the
first month will most likely look like nothing happened. What it produces is inventory
and a repeatable habit — and, on Shorts specifically, a searchable back catalogue that
keeps working after the post stops. The reason to run it is that it is the only
discovery channel that does not depend on somebody else saying yes.
