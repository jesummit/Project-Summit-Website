**Date:** 2026-08-04 · **Constraint:** discovery, not conversion. The product is embryonic; there is nothing downstream worth optimising until people know it exists.

Named targets and angles, not frameworks. Every venue below was verified to exist and be active.

## Tier 2 — Where the ICP already discusses the exact problem

Participation, not link-dropping. The rule that keeps this from backfiring: **be useful ten times before mentioning the product once**, and disclose that you built it every single time.

**English**
- **TrainerRoad forum** — the richest source of ICP language found in the VOC research. Threads on HRV, sleep scores and recovery-driven training are already running.
- **r/Velo** — serious amateurs, power meters, structured training. The exact ICP.
- **r/cycling** — broader, larger, lower intent.

**Spanish** — the Instagram data says the audience is Spanish-dominant, and the ES blog out-reads the English originals, yet none of this is worked:
- **ForoCiclista** — has a dedicated *Entrenamiento basado en Potencia* section. The closest single match to the ICP in Spanish.
- **ForoMTB** — large, active, with running power-training threads.
- **MTBeros** — growing, MTB and e-MTB.

Reddit's public JSON API refuses unauthenticated access, so r/Velo could not be sampled for the VOC research. It still belongs on this list; it just needs a logged-in human.

| Spanish cycling media | Maillot/Ciclismo a Fondo-type outlets — same play, in the language the audience actually uses. |

### The two angles that make this a story rather than a press release

**1. "Free recovery and sleep from the watch you already own."** Whoop charges a subscription for the recovery score; Summit gives the score, the full sleep breakdown, the trend and the widgets away permanently. The VOC research found subscription fatigue is one of the loudest, most repeated complaints in this exact category — *"Great tracking awful price"*, *"the price is steep at $30/mo"*. That is a pitch a tech reviewer can build a segment around, and it is verifiably true.

**2. "Not AI. Deterministic and explainable."** The VOC research found the category is actively punishing AI-forward positioning right now — TrainerRoad's AI overhaul and WHOOP's assistant are generating the angriest reviews in the corpus. A training app that deliberately refuses the AI label, and can trace every recommendation to a codified rule, is a contrarian story with live news pegs.

Both are true, both are differentiated, and neither requires a budget.

## Tier 4 — One-shot launch surfaces

**Product Hunt** is the anchor, and it needs three weeks of preparation, not a submission. Warm the account, build the Upcoming page, launch 12:01 AM Pacific on a Tuesday–Thursday, post the founder's story as the first comment, reply to everything within 30 minutes. **Ask for feedback, never for upvotes** — the algorithm penalises solicitation, and 80% of failed launches fail from launching cold.

Do not run this until there is something to land on. It is a spike, and a spike into a homepage with no comparison pages and no reviews wastes the one launch the product gets.

---

## What not to do yet

- **Paid ads.** Nothing to scale.
- **Mass directory submission.** Most of the standard catalog is for B2B SaaS, and the readiness gate is not met (no comparison pages, no demo video).
- **Cold outreach to strangers on Reddit or Product Hunt.** Gets flagged, and the community is small enough that reputational damage is durable.

## Honest caveat

Nothing here produces a step change on its own. Community work compounds over months and press coverage is a coin flip on whether a given reviewer bites. The reason to start with the intervals.icu thread is not that it is big — it is that it already worked once, by accident, with a single post.
