Plans

What's free, and what Premium adds.

Your daily recovery and your full sleep analysis are free for as long as you use Summit. Premium adds the training layer on top of that.

Free
Free forever · not a trial
  • Daily recovery score — from your HRV, resting heart rate and sleep, read from Apple Health.
  • Sleep score with the full breakdown — how last night compares to your own historical median, sleep debt, stages and rhythm.
  • Recovery trend — how your recovery is moving, not just today's number.
  • Home and Lock Screen widgets — your numbers without opening the app.

Any wearable that writes HRV and sleep to Apple Health works. An Apple Watch is not required.

Pro
Everything free, plus the training layer
  • Training load — CTL, ATL and TSB, live across every session.
  • Power analysis — eFTP and your power curve, estimated continuously from your ride history. No manual tests.
  • Per-activity detailed analysis — power, heart rate, elevation and zones, ride by ride.
  • Progression tracking and the effort calculator.
  • Race weather for your target events.
  • Notifications — eFTP change, form peak, race-day fuelling and your weekly recap.
Elite
Everything in Pro, plus the adaptive plan
  • The adaptive training plan — periodisation that rewrites itself around your recovery and the time you actually have.
  • Peak-form adaptation — the plan reshapes around the events in your calendar.
  • Workout export — ZWO and FIT files, plus intervals.icu sync out to Garmin, Zwift and MyWhoosh.
  • Race-day nutrition plan — carbs, fluid and sodium matched to your event's duration and your bodyweight.
  • Notifications — today's plan and your weekly availability reminder.

Daily training-driven nutrition, with recipes and a shopping list, is in development and not part of the current App Store release.

The free tier is not the trial

Summit Premium comes with a 14-day free trial. That is a separate thing. Your recovery score, your sleep score and its breakdown, the trend and the widgets stay free whether or not you ever start a trial, and they do not expire when one ends.

Where to see the price

We don't publish figures here. Apple sets Premium pricing per country and it can change, so the only number worth trusting is the live one: you'll see it on the App Store listing and inside the app before anything is charged. Monthly and yearly are both available.
